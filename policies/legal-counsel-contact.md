## Escalation Criteria

**Call legal counsel immediately if:**

1. **PII data breach suspected**
   - Customer data accessed without authorization
   - KYC/AML data exposed
   - Identity documents compromised

2. **Payment data breach suspected**
   - Credit card numbers accessed
   - Payment processor credentials compromised
   - PCI DSS scope expanded unexpectedly

3. **Regulatory inquiry received**
   - VDAI (Lithuanian DPA) contact
   - Payment brand (Visa/Mastercard) inquiry
   - Law enforcement request

4. **Ransomware or extortion**
   - Systems encrypted by attacker
   - Attacker demanding payment
   - Data theft with threat of publication

## Communication Protocol

### During Incident

1. **Initial contact:** Email or phone (whichever is faster)
2. **Information to provide:**
   - Incident severity (P1/P2/P3/P4)
   - Brief description (what happened)
   - Data potentially affected (PII, payment, etc.)
   - Current containment status
   - Immediate questions

3. **Expected response:**
   - Acknowledgment within 30 minutes
   - Initial guidance within 2 hours (P1)
   - Ongoing availability until incident contained

### After Incident

1. **Post-incident review:** Schedule within 5 business days
2. **Deliverables:**
   - Legal review of incident record
   - Regulatory notification assessment
   - Lessons learned (legal perspective)
   - Updated compliance recommendations
